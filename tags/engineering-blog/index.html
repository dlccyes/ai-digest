    GitHub’s hardest repositories exposed a subtle distributed-systems bottleneck: a quorum can require only a majority to commit and still be throttled by every healthy replica. GitHub’s existing storage layer waited for all healthy replicas before releasing a repository-wide write lock. Once replicas were split between its data centers and Azure, a roughly 25-millisecond cross-region round trip sat inside every serialized write.
